# Retail-Customer-Behaviour-Sales-Analysis
This project analyses customer purchasing behaviour for a fast-growing retail company operating across online and mall-based channels, with products spanning fashion, cosmetics, food and beverage, footwear, and other retail categories.

Using customer purchase and transaction data across multiple malls, the analysis explores customer behaviour, purchasing patterns, product performance, and sales trends to identify opportunities for improving customer engagement, sales, and loyalty.

The project applies Python, pandas, statistical analysis, and data visualisation to transform raw transaction data into actionable business insights. The findings are used to develop targeted sales and marketing recommendations, including opportunities to better understand customer segments, identify high-value behaviours, and improve promotional strategies.
